What is Karma Coins?

Karma Points

Your Lifetime record. Only ever goes up, Spending never touches it.

Karma Coins

Your spendable balance. Use it on marketplace courses.

How Karma Coins work

Every Karma Point you earn also earns you Karma Coin. The points stay as a permanent records of what you achieved. The coins are what you actually spends.
